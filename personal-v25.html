PANEL ATLM-ANOMPYTHON · PYTORCHCALIBRATING

Satellite Telemetry Anomaly Detection

PYTHONPYTORCHVAEGMMPCAK-MEANS

Trained on real ESA spacecraft telemetry archives (power, thermal, attitude, communications channels, 10M+ records). Conv1D, Hierarchical and Transformer VAEs plus Gaussian Mixture Models, PCA and K-Means learn nominal onboard operation, so the system flags a subsystem drifting toward failure in real time.

LIVE DEMO · 4 simulated channels at 30 Hz. The console calibrates per-channel residual σ against a nominal model, then gates on a rolling RMS z-score > 3σ. Inject a slow drift and watch it get caught.
