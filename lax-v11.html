Chapter 04 — The League

Great Lakes.
Great rivals.

Club Lacrosse Team playing other top club lacrosse programs in the North West region through the Great Lakes Lacrosse League (GLLL). The GLLL is a regional, non-varsity men's club field lacrosse league — 40+ programs across Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Michigan, Minnesota, the Dakotas, and Wisconsin.

We compete in Division 2. In 2024 we ran the table and brought the D2 title home from Madison. Our mission hasn't changed: to play and teach the game of lacrosse at a collegiate level.
