Zero libraries · raw WebGL

A fluid
made of math.

No video, no three.js, no shader library — this is a 20-line GLSL fragment shader compiled and run directly against the browser's WebGL context.

It follows
your cursor.

Mouse position is passed in as a uniform every frame; scroll position warps the field's frequency.

Still 60fps.

The entire effect runs on the GPU — the CPU only ever updates three numbers per frame.
