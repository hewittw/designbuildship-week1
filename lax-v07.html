Chicago wins D2 title, 11–0

Club Maroons run the table in the Great Lakes league, carry the trophy home from Madison — and the door to the roster is still wide open

By Ledger Sports StaffClub Lacrosse · GLLL D2

Chicago — There was no twelfth game to win, so the University of Chicago men’s club lacrosse team did the next best thing: it took the trophy home. Chicago closed out the 2024 Great Lakes Lacrosse League season at 11–0, claiming the Division 2 championship at the league tournament in Madison, Wis.

The program is a student-run club, not a varsity team. It plays a spring schedule against the top non-varsity programs of the upper Midwest through the GLLL, a league of roughly 40 teams stretching from Illinois and Indiana to Minnesota and the Dakotas.

The record held up under pressure. Chicago won by an average of better than four goals, but two of its biggest wins came by one: an 8–7 overtime game against Wisconsin–La Crosse in March, then a 9–8 rematch with the same program in the tournament semifinal. The final, against Wisconsin–Whitewater, finished 11–7.

The club credits the run to the same three words it puts on everything: fun, camaraderie, grit. (See The Creed, Page C2.)

Unlike most champions, Chicago isn’t protecting its roster. Under the club’s constitution, membership is open to any UChicago student, undergraduate or graduate, of any major or division. There are no cuts. The club’s stated purpose is “to play and teach the game of lacrosse at a collegiate level,” and it means the teaching part literally.

Fall is the off-season: conditioning, recruiting and league business, starting with the GLLL meeting on Sunday, Nov. 1, where member clubs work out spring scheduling, officiating and logistics. The season opens in March and builds to the Division 2 tournament weekend in Madison in mid-April.

The GLLL, explained in 60 seconds

What is it?
The Great Lakes Lacrosse League is a regional, non-varsity men’s club field lacrosse league with roughly 40 teams across the upper Midwest.
Where?
  • ILL.
  • IND.
  • IOWA
  • MICH.
  • MINN.
  • N.D.
  • S.D.
  • WIS.
Who plays?
Founding and notable programs include Notre Dame, Loyola Chicago, Carleton, St. Norbert, Wisconsin, Wisconsin–La Crosse, Wisconsin–Platteville, Wisconsin–Whitewater and Milwaukee’s Mad Cow Lacrosse Club.
Where does Chicago fit?
Division 2. Chicago won the D2 title in 2024 with an 11–0 record, the program’s headline achievement.
When?
Spring, roughly March through April, ending with the league tournament in Madison, Wis. Fall is for meetings, conditioning and sign-ups.

The Creed · C2

Three words that went 11–0

To this club, fun, camaraderie and grit aren’t a slogan on a T-shirt. They describe how the team is run.

No. 1

Fun

Nobody has to play club lacrosse, so everyone there chose to. Practices are competitive, but the bus rides, the sideline chirping and the trophy photo with a T-shirt over the shoulder pads are the reason people come back.

No. 2

Camaraderie

Undergrads and grad students, first-time stick holders and four-year starters all share one roster. A student board of captains and officers runs the club, so the players are the ones running the team.

No. 3

Grit

Talent alone doesn’t produce eleven straight wins. Fall conditioning and cold early-spring practices helped Chicago win both of its one-goal games in 2024, including the overtime game in March and the semifinal in Madison.

Road to Madison

Last stop on the road: a lakeside stadium in Wisconsin’s capital

Every GLLL season ends in Madison, where the league holds its tournament. In 2024, Chicago played three games there in two days and won all three. The 2027 tournament is scheduled for mid-April, and the team will be decided by whoever signs up this fall.
