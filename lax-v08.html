    The Great Lakes Lacrosse League is a regional non-varsity men's club field lacrosse league — roughly 40+ teams across Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Michigan, Minnesota, the Dakotas, and Wisconsin. Notable programs include Notre Dame, Loyola Chicago, Carleton, St. Norbert, Wisconsin, UW–La Crosse, UW–Platteville, UW–Whitewater, and Milwaukee's Mad Cow Lacrosse Club.

    03 / ON THE BOARD
    Crowd prompts · team identity

    Fun. Camaraderie. Grit.

    Three words the club actually runs on — lit up the way a stadium ribbon board asks the crowd to get loud. Hover a board.

    PROMPT 01
    Make some noise

    Fun first

    Club lacrosse is a choice you make on top of a UChicago course load. We keep it loud, loose, and worth showing up for — road trips, Saturday games, and a sideline that never goes quiet.

    PROMPT 02
    Everybody in

    Camaraderie

    Undergrads and grad students, first-timers and four-year starters, on the same bus. Led by a student board of captains and officers, built by everyone who suits up.

    PROMPT 03
    Defense · defense

    Grit

    Eleven games, eleven wins, and a trophy from Madison. Grit is the 7 a.m. lift, the ground ball nobody else wants, and teaching a rookie to play the game of lacrosse at a collegiate level.
