The Great
Lakes League

We play top club lacrosse programs in the North West region through the Great Lakes Lacrosse League (GLLL), a non-varsity men's club field lacrosse league with 40+ teams across eight states in the Midwest and the Dakotas. We compete in Division 2, and we're the 2024 D2 Champions.
