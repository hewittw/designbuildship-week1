Program Pillars

"To play and teach the game of lacrosse at a collegiate level."
01

Fun

It is a club. Practices are competitive, but nobody is on scholarship and nobody forgets why they showed up. Bus rides to Wisconsin are half the point.

02

Camaraderie

Undergrads and grad students, veterans and first-timers, from every corner of campus. One sideline, one team, one group chat.

03

Grit

Early-season games in Midwest sleet. Ground balls you have no business winning. That is how a club team goes 11–0.

About the GLLL

theglll.com ↗

UChicago Men's Club Lacrosse plays other top club lacrosse programs in the North West region through the Great Lakes Lacrosse League.

The GLLL is a regional, non-varsity men's club field lacrosse league with 40+ teams. Chicago competes in Division 2, where it finished the 2024 season a perfect 11–0 and claimed the D2 championship at the league tournament in Madison, Wisconsin.

Membership in MLAX is open to any UChicago student, undergraduate or graduate, regardless of major or division, consistent with the University's non-discrimination policy.
