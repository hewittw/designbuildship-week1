01   The 2024 seasonUndefeated

11–0

2024 GLLL D2 Champion

Eleven games. Eleven wins. One trophy, back to Hyde Park.

In 2024, UChicago Men's Club Lacrosse ran the table in the Great Lakes Lacrosse League and finished as Division 2 Champions, closing the season at the league tournament in Madison, Wisconsin.

No varsity budget. No cuts. Just students who kept showing up — and a standard the next roster gets to defend.

02   What we stand forThree words
  • Fun.
  • Camaraderie.
  • Grit.

Fun 01

We play because we love the game. Practices are competitive, sidelines are loud, and nobody takes themselves too seriously off the field.

Camaraderie 02

Undergrads and grad students. First-time players and lifelong middies. One roster, one bus to Madison, one team.

Grit 03

Fall conditioning, early-spring practices in Chicago wind, and an undefeated season that came from both. We earn it.

03   The teamCamaraderie, documented

04   The leagueGLLL

Great Lakes Lacrosse League.

We play other top club programs across the North West region through the Great Lakes Lacrosse League — a non-varsity men’s club field lacrosse league spanning the Upper Midwest.

40+Member teams
D2Our division
2024Champions

The league reaches from Illinois and Indiana to Iowa, Michigan, Minnesota, the Dakotas and Wisconsin. Notable programs include:

  • University of Notre Dame IN
  • Loyola University Chicago IL
  • Carleton College MN
  • St. Norbert College WI
  • University of Wisconsin WI
  • Wisconsin–La Crosse WI
  • Wisconsin–Platteville WI
  • Wisconsin–Whitewater WI
  • Mad Cow Lacrosse Club Milwaukee
  • UChicago D2 Champs
