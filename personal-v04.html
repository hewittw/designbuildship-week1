Satellite Telemetry Anomaly Detection

Unsupervised anomaly detection trained on real ESA (European Space Agency) spacecraft telemetry archives — power, thermal, attitude, and communications channels — spanning 10M+ records. Conv1D, Hierarchical, and Transformer Variational Autoencoders learn a compressed latent manifold of nominal operation, refined with Gaussian Mixture Models, PCA, and K-Means clustering, so the instant an onboard subsystem starts drifting toward failure, it falls outside the trained manifold and gets flagged in real time.
