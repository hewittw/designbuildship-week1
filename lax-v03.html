The leagueDivision 2

Great Lakes Lacrosse League

The GLLL is a regional, non-varsity men’s club field lacrosse league — roughly 40+ programs across Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Michigan, Minnesota, the Dakotas, and Wisconsin. We compete in Division 2, and in 2024 we finished the season 11–0 and brought home the D2 title from the league tournament in Madison, WI.
