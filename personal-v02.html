Trained on real ESA (European Space Agency) spacecraft telemetry archives — power, thermal, attitude, and communications channels — across 10M+ records. An unsupervised pipeline combining Conv1D, Hierarchical, and Transformer VAEs with Gaussian Mixture Models, PCA, and K-Means learns what "nominal" onboard operation looks like, so it can flag the moment a subsystem starts drifting toward failure, in real time.
