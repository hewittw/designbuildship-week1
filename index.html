Part 1 — Personal site, featured

Same resume, same two headshots, same three dogs — thirteen executions, each either a real app/brand clone (Instagram, iMessage, Notes, Netflix, Quizlet, Amazon) or a fully-realized theme (ocean waves, FNAF security office, All Blacks, darkroom, circuit board, live binary, SpaceX launch) as the delivery vehicle for the same content. These are the strongest of the 25 personal versions — the other twelve, the initial wide exploration, are archived at the bottom as Personal — First Attempt.
