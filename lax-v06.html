Gallery IV

The Three Virtues

Every program has a trophy case. Ours has a creed. Three objects, each on its own plinth, each lit from above.

Fun

gaudium — the first reason anyone picks up a stick

Beginners and lifers on the same field. Wall-ball after class, road trips to Wisconsin, a sideline that never goes quiet.

Camaraderie

fraternitas — undergraduates and grad students, one roster

Every division, every major. The photo on the far wall is the proof: you win as a team or you do not win at all.

Grit

constantia — Chicago winters, early lifts, ground balls

Eleven wins do not happen by accident. They happen in February on a cold turf field, one rep at a time.

Gallery V

Provenance

Where the hardware comes from: the Great Lakes Lacrosse League.

The Great Lakes Lacrosse League is a regional, non-varsity men’s club field lacrosse league — more than forty programs spread across Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Michigan, Minnesota, the Dakotas, and Wisconsin. Its season is short and honest: a spring campaign from March into April, ending at the league tournament in Madison.

UChicago competes in the league’s Division 2, against club programs from the region’s best universities and colleges. In 2024 we went through that division without a loss.
