About the GLLL

Men's club
field lacrosse

The GLLL is the regional non-varsity men's club field lacrosse league for the upper Midwest, with roughly 40+ teams. It handles scheduling, officiating, and the spring tournament, and the whole league meets to coordinate it all.

UChicago competes in Division 2, and in 2024 we finished it 11–0 as D2 Champions. Our mission, straight from the RSO registration: “To play and teach the game of lacrosse at a collegiate level.”
