§ 01 — The Play

How we ran it
eleven straight.

01 Faceoff · win the draw

Rule one: Fun.

Every possession starts with a scrum at the X. We play hard because we like playing — the van rides, the pregame playlist, the practice that turns into a shooting contest. Our midfielder scoops it and goes.

02 Swing · move the ball

Nobody scores alone. Camaraderie.

Top → behind → wing → top → behind. Undergrads and grad students, first-timers and four-year starters — one sideline, and everyone touches the ball before it goes in.

03 Pick & dodge · finish

Take the hit. Grit.

A pick topside, a defender who slides a step late, and someone willing to absorb a check to get the shot off. Ground balls are a choice. So is showing up to winter conditioning.

Know the league

The GLLL

The Great Lakes Lacrosse League is the regional, non-varsity men's club field lacrosse league for the upper Midwest — roughly 40+ programs across Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Michigan, Minnesota, the Dakotas, and Wisconsin. It runs a spring season that ends with a league tournament in Madison, WI.

  • DivisionDivision 2 (D2)
  • 2024 record11–0 · D2 Champions
  • SeasonSpring (March–April); fall is recruiting, conditioning, and league meetings
  • Home turfUChicago turf, Stagg Field area
