Membership is open to any UChicago student, undergraduate or graduate, regardless of major or division.

That line comes straight from our constitution and the University’s non-discrimination policy. Our purpose, as registered with campus: “To play and teach the game of lacrosse at a collegiate level.” Teach is the operative word. We’ll hand you a stick.

Three
Articles.

The whole program runs on three words. We’ll ask you to bring all of them — and we’ll give all three back.

No. IMLAX

Fun.

Club lacrosse is the best version of the sport: competitive enough to matter, loose enough to laugh. Road trips, Stagg Field Saturdays, a team that actually likes each other.

No. IIMLAX

Camara­derie.

First-years next to PhD students. Day-one beginners next to four-year varsity guys. The roster is a cross-section of the whole university, and that’s the point.

No. IIIMLAX

Grit.

Eleven games, eleven wins, one trophy carried off the field in Madison. That record was built on cold fall conditioning and early spring practices. It still is.

This is the 2024 squad and the hardware they brought home. Look closely and you’ll find one guy who wore an “If you’re not first, you’re last” T-shirt over his gear. He was right. Next spring there’s room in this photo for you.

The League

The
Great
Lakes.

We compete in the Great Lakes Lacrosse League (GLLL), a regional men’s club field lacrosse league of 40+ non-varsity programs across Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Michigan, Minnesota, the Dakotas and Wisconsin. Chicago plays in Division 2, and as of 2024 we are its champions.
